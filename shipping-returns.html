Shipping & Returns

Delivery & Return Information

We're committed to getting your order to you quickly and hassle-free. Learn about our shipping options, delivery times, and return process.

Last Updated: March 15, 2024

1. Shipping Policy

Delivery Areas

We currently deliver to the following areas:

  • Local Delivery: Hammanskraal and surrounding areas
  • National Delivery: Main cities and towns across South Africa

Shipping Costs

Shipping costs are calculated at checkout based on your delivery location:

  • Local Delivery: R60 (Hammanskraal and surrounding areas)
  • National Delivery: R120 (outside Hammanskraal area)

Note: Shipping costs may vary for remote areas. You will see the exact cost at checkout before completing your purchase.

Processing Time

All items are made to order and require production time before shipping:

  • Ready-to-Wear Items: 2-3 business days processing time
  • Custom Tailored Items: 2-4 weeks processing time (depending on complexity)
  • Express Processing: Available for an additional fee (contact us for details)

Delivery Time

Once your order is processed, delivery times are as follows:

  • Local Delivery: 1-2 business days after processing
  • National Delivery: 3-5 business days after processing

Delivery times may be affected by public holidays, weather conditions, or other unforeseen circumstances.

Order Tracking

Once your order ships, you will receive a confirmation email with tracking information. You can track your package using the provided tracking number with our delivery partner.

Delivery Instructions

To ensure successful delivery:

  • Provide a complete and accurate delivery address
  • Include a contact phone number for the delivery person
  • Specify any special delivery instructions in the order notes
  • Ensure someone is available to receive the package during business hours

2. Returns & Exchanges

Return Eligibility

We want you to be completely satisfied with your purchase. You may return items that meet the following criteria:

  • Items must be in new, unworn condition with all original tags attached
  • Items must be returned within 14 days of delivery
  • Items must include all original packaging and documentation
  • Custom tailored items cannot be returned unless there is a manufacturing defect

Non-Returnable Items

The following items cannot be returned for hygiene and safety reasons:

  • Custom made or personalized items
  • Items that show signs of wear or damage
  • Items without original tags or packaging
  • Items returned after 14 days from delivery

Return Process

To initiate a return, please follow these steps:

  1. Contact our customer service at purpleray23@gmail.com within 14 days of delivery
  2. Provide your order number and reason for the return
  3. We will provide you with return instructions and authorization
  4. Pack the item securely in its original packaging
  5. Ship the item back to us using the provided instructions

Return Shipping

Return shipping costs are the responsibility of the customer unless the return is due to:

  • Wrong item shipped
  • Defective or damaged item
  • Our error in fulfillment

We recommend using a trackable shipping method and purchasing shipping insurance for valuable items.

Refunds

Once we receive your returned item, we will process your refund within 5-7 business days:

  • Refunds will be issued to the original payment method
  • You will receive a confirmation email once the refund is processed
  • Shipping costs are non-refundable except in cases of our error
  • Custom items are not eligible for refunds unless defective

3. Exchanges

Exchange Policy

We offer exchanges for items that meet our return criteria:

  • Exchanges are subject to availability of the new item
  • You may need to pay any price difference for the new item
  • Exchange shipping costs are the responsibility of the customer
  • Custom items cannot be exchanged unless defective

How to Request an Exchange

To request an exchange:

  1. Contact customer service within 14 days of delivery
  2. Specify the item you wish to exchange and the new item you want
  3. We will confirm availability and provide exchange instructions
  4. Return the original item following our return process
  5. We will ship the new item once the original is received

4. Damaged or Defective Items

What to Do

If you receive a damaged or defective item:

  • Inspect your order upon delivery
  • Take photos of any damage or defects
  • Contact us immediately at purpleray23@gmail.com
  • Provide your order number and photos of the issue

Our Commitment

We will resolve any quality issues promptly:

  • Free return shipping for defective items
  • Full refund or replacement at your preference
  • Priority handling for quality issues
  • Investigation to prevent future occurrences

5. Custom Tailoring Specifics

Measurements & Fit

For custom tailored items:

  • Accurate measurements are the customer's responsibility
  • We are not liable for garments that don't fit due to incorrect measurements
  • Minor fit adjustments may be available for an additional fee
  • Custom items cannot be returned or exchanged unless defective

Production Issues

If there are issues with custom garment production:

  • Contact us within 7 days of receiving the item
  • We will assess the issue and provide solutions
  • Repair, remake, or refund options available for defects
  • Additional fittings may be required for adjustments

6. International Shipping

Currently, we only ship within South Africa. For international inquiries, please contact us at purpleray23@gmail.com to discuss options and pricing.
