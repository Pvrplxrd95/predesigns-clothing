Last Updated: March 15, 2024

How to Measure Yourself

For the most accurate measurements, wear minimal clothing and use a soft measuring tape. If possible, have someone assist you with taking measurements.

Chest/Bust

Measure around the fullest part of your chest, keeping the tape parallel to the floor. Make sure the tape is not too tight or too loose.

Tip: For women, measure over a bra that you would normally wear.

Waist

Measure around your natural waistline, which is the narrowest part of your torso, usually just above the belly button.

Tip: Keep the tape snug but not tight. Don't suck in your stomach.

Hips

Measure around the fullest part of your hips and buttocks, keeping the tape parallel to the floor.

Tip: Stand with your feet together for the most accurate measurement.

Inseam

Measure from the top of your inner leg down to where you want the pants to end (ankle, mid-calf, etc.).

Tip: You can also measure an existing pair of well-fitting pants from crotch to hem.

Shoulder Width

Measure from the edge of one shoulder to the edge of the other, across the back.

Tip: Find the bony point at the edge of your shoulder for accurate measurement.

Sleeve Length

Measure from the edge of your shoulder (where the sleeve starts) down to your wrist bone.

Tip: Bend your arm slightly for a more natural measurement.

Fitting Tips

Streetwear Fit

Our streetwear collection is designed with a relaxed, comfortable fit. If you prefer a more fitted look, consider sizing down.

Custom Tailoring

For custom-made garments, provide your exact measurements. We'll create a pattern specifically for your body type.

Layering

Consider what you'll wear underneath when choosing sizes for jackets and outerwear.

Stretch Fabrics

Items with stretch content may fit more snugly. If between sizes, consider sizing down.

Custom Tailoring Measurements

For custom garments, we require more detailed measurements. When ordering custom clothing, we'll provide a comprehensive measurement guide and may require a fitting session.

Required measurements for custom items typically include:

  • Chest/Bust
  • Waist
  • Hips
  • Shoulder width
  • Sleeve length
  • Chest to waist
  • Waist to knee
  • Outseam (waist to ankle)
  • Neck circumference
  • Bicep circumference
  • Thigh circumference
