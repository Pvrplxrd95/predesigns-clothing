About Us

Crafted with Passion, Designed with Purpose

Founded in 2019 by Josias Tlou, Predesigns Clothing is more than just a fashion brand - it's a movement. Based in Hammanskraal, North of Pretoria, we specialize in luxury streetwear that speaks to the soul while maintaining the highest standards of craftsmanship.

Our expertise extends beyond streetwear to include custom-tailored traditional clothing, sophisticated suits, elegant dresses, and stunning gowns. Every piece is made from scratch with meticulous attention to detail and infused with local love and taste.

Predesigns Clothing is a proud brand under the Purple Ray Group umbrella.
