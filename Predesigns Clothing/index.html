About Us

Crafted with Passion, Designed with Purpose

Founded in 2019 by Josias Tlou, Predesigns Clothing is more than just a fashion brand — it's a movement. Based in Hammanskraal, North of Pretoria, we specialise in urban tailoring — streetwear engineered for fit, not just fashion. Every piece is made from scratch with meticulous attention to detail and infused with local love and taste.

Our expertise extends beyond streetwear to include custom-tailored traditional clothing, sophisticated suits, elegant dresses, and stunning gowns. Every piece is made from scratch with meticulous attention to detail and infused with local love and taste.
